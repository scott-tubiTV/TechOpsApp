How It Works
1️⃣
Upload CSV
Upload a partner title list in CSV format with title name, director, cast, country, and license dates.
2️⃣
Auto-Detect
The system fuzzy-matches against the master catalog to find duplicates (same title, different source) and conflicts (overlapping licenses in same country).
3️⃣
Resolve
Review flagged titles and choose to keep existing, replace, keep both, or skip. Resolutions are logged for audit.
